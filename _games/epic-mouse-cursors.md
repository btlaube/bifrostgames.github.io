Complete your office workday while surviving glitches caused by a suspiciously “free” EPIC Mouse Cursor Pack! Read emails, update your quarterly report, and race against the clock as unexpected interruptions put your mouse skills to the test.

---

# How to Play

Read your boss's email and follow the instructions to complete your quarterly report.

Navigate your desktop, open files, and submit your finished work.

Survive glitch interruptions by playing fast-paced, mouse-controlled minigames.

Beat the 5 PM deadline to finish your workday!

> Note: Only one minigame is available for now, with more planned for the future.
