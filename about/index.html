About

Hi there, I'm Ben Laube.

My love of video games began in childhood, inspired by the way games spark imagination and pull you into entirely new worlds. Over time that fascination turned into a commitment to building interactive experiences of my own.

I've been developing games in Unity for over five years, from small experimental prototypes to published releases. My goal is to create games that are not only fun to play but meaningful and memorable long after the credits roll.

The craft

How I make games

Whether I'm implementing a complex mechanic, trying a new tool or refining the feel of a system, I try to blend creativity with technical precision, and I'm always looking for ways to create innovative, immersive experiences that resonate with players.

About BifrostGames

Where the name comes from

BifrostGames is my development identity and the home of my game collection, built on a gamer tag I've used since I was 13.

Age 13

A gamer tag is born

The name started as the gamer tag I made when I got my first PlayStation 3. I was deep into Marvel movies, and Heimdall from the Thor films was my favorite character.

Norse mythology

Guardian of the Bifrost

Heimdall guards the Bifrost, the Asgardian bridge between realms. Wanting a name inspired by him, I chose Bifrost_Guardian, misspelling and all, since 13-year-old me couldn't spell the word. It became part of my online persona.

Fall 2022

A brand takes shape

In my junior year of college I started seriously considering a game development company. Step one was a brand, somewhere between a personal pseudonym and a studio name. BifrostGames now stands for both my games and my development identity.
